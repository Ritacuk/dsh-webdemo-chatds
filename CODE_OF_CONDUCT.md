# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith in disagreements; critique ideas, not people. Harassment, personal attacks, and spam are not tolerated.

## Scope

This applies to the repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and any community spaces operated for this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, and block accounts that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations lead to a permanent ban.

## Attribution

This standard follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; it is intentionally brief rather than a verbatim copy.
